A little background

The whole stack.
The same curiosity.

I'm a developer, software architect and mentor based in Sweden. My programming journey has taken me from Motorola 68000 and x86 assembly through C, C++, Java, C#, JavaScript and Python.

Low-level optimization, algorithms and data structures are as interesting to me as software architecture and system design. Earlier in my career, I lived and breathed real-time 3D rendering and GPU programming.

These days, much of my work centres on data, machine learning, AI and agent-first development practices. I still make time for graphics, simulations, music and anything that just seems cool. Some projects solve a problem; others start with “I wonder if…”.
